Installation process

Seven steps from bare slab to finished floor.

Every project is different, but a professional installation follows the same order. Preparation matters as much as the coating itself.

1

Evaluate

We inspect the slab, cracks, old coatings and contamination, and talk through how you use the space.

2

Choose a system

Pick colors, flake blends, textures and the coating system that fits.

3

Prepare

Mechanical grinding removes contaminants and opens the surface for a strong bond.

4

Repair

Cracks, pits and divots are filled so the finish goes down over a sound surface.

5

Apply

Primer, base coat and any flakes or metallic pigments go down in order.

6

Topcoat

A protective finish adds abrasion and chemical resistance and sets the sheen.

7

Cure

We tell you when to walk on it, move things back, and park again.

Ask every contractor this
A premium coating can't make up for poor surface prep.

When you compare estimates, don't just compare products. Ask how the floor will be prepared.

  • Grinding, not just etchingDiamond grinding creates the surface profile coatings need to bond.
  • Contaminant removalOil, grease, curing compounds and old coatings are taken off first.
  • Crack and spall repairDamaged areas are filled so the finish lies flat and sound.
  • Moisture checkVapor coming through the slab can cause adhesion problems later.

Ask a Question →
How long does epoxy flooring last?
It depends on the system, the prep, the environment and how the floor is used. A home garage and a busy warehouse put very different demands on a floor, so we size the system to the job.
Can you coat over cracked concrete?
Most cracks and damaged areas can be repaired before coating. Concrete can keep moving over time, so future movement may still show through. We assess this during the evaluation.
Can you install over an old coating?
Sometimes. We check the existing coating's condition and adhesion. Often it needs to be ground off or abraded before the new system goes down.
Will it hold up in Arizona heat?
With the right system and prep, yes. For sun-exposed garages and patios we typically recommend UV-stable polyaspartic topcoats, and good grinding helps prevent hot-tire pickup.
Is epoxy flooring slippery?
Any smooth floor can be slippery when wet. We can add texture or aggregate for traction. More texture means more grip; smoother floors are easier to mop. We'll help you find the balance.
Can I pick my own colors?
Yes. Depending on the system, there are many solid colors, flake blends and metallic options to choose from.
How much does epoxy flooring cost in El Mirage?
Price depends on square footage, slab condition, prep, repairs, the coating system and decorative options. The most accurate number comes from looking at your floor, so request a free estimate.
